Shared web hosting is a solution where multiple websites are hosted on the same physical server. It's the most economical and popular option for small websites and blogs. Resources like CPU, RAM, and disk space are shared among all users on the server.

Shared hosting uses a server shared with other users, making it more affordable but with limited resources. A VPS (Virtual Private Server) provides dedicated resources and better control over your hosting environment, offering more power and customization options.
